Free Online Word Counter

FreeToolsHub Word Counter is a simple and fast online tool that helps you count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs in your text. It is useful for students, writers, bloggers, content creators, marketers and anyone who needs to check text length.

How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. The word count will update automatically.
  3. Check characters, sentences and paragraphs.
  4. Use the reading time to estimate how long your text takes to read.
  5. Click Copy Text if you want to copy your text.
  6. Click Clear to remove the text and start again.

What Does This Word Counter Calculate?

  • Words: Total number of words in your text.
  • Characters: Total characters including spaces.
  • Characters Without Spaces: Characters excluding whitespace.
  • Sentences: Approximate number of sentences based on punctuation.
  • Paragraphs: Number of non-empty paragraphs.
  • Reading Time: Estimated reading time based on approximately 200 words per minute.

Who Can Use a Word Counter?

This tool can be useful for students writing assignments, bloggers checking article length, social media creators preparing captions, copywriters, SEO professionals, teachers and writers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this word counter free?

Yes. The FreeToolsHub Word Counter is completely free to use.

Does the word count update automatically?

Yes. The statistics update instantly while you type or paste text.

Does it count characters with spaces?

Yes. The tool provides both total characters and characters excluding spaces.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated using approximately 200 words per minute. Actual reading speed varies from person to person.

Can I use this tool for essays and assignments?

Yes. You can paste your essay, assignment, article or any other text to quickly check its length.
