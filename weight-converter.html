What is Weight Conversion?

Weight conversion is the process of changing a measurement from one unit of weight or mass to another. Common units include kilograms, grams, milligrams, tonnes, pounds and ounces.

This free weight converter makes it easy to convert measurements without manually calculating conversion factors.

Supported Weight Units

  • Kilogram (kg)
  • Gram (g)
  • Milligram (mg)
  • Tonne (t)
  • Pound (lb)
  • Ounce (oz)
