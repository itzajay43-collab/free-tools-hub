What is a Percentage?

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. The word percentage means "per hundred." Percentages are commonly used in discounts, taxes, interest rates, exams, business calculations, statistics, finance and everyday calculations.

Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

For example, if you scored 80 marks out of 100:

(80 ÷ 100) × 100 = 80%

How to Calculate Percentage of a Number

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the percentage by the number and divide the result by 100.

Result = (Percentage × Number) ÷ 100

Example: 20% of 500 is:

(20 × 500) ÷ 100 = 100

Percentage Increase Formula

Percentage Increase = ((New Value − Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100

Example: If a price increases from 100 to 120:

((120 − 100) ÷ 100) × 100 = 20%

Percentage Decrease Formula

Percentage Decrease = ((Old Value − New Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100

Example: If a price decreases from 200 to 150:

((200 − 150) ÷ 200) × 100 = 25%

Percentage Difference Formula

Percentage Difference = |A − B| ÷ ((A + B) ÷ 2) × 100

Percentage difference is useful when comparing two values and neither value is considered the original or reference value.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply the result by 100. For example, 25 out of 100 is 25%.

What is 20% of 500?

20% of 500 is 100.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100.

How do I calculate percentage decrease?

Subtract the new value from the old value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100.
