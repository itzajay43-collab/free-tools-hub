What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is a free online tool that calculates a person's age from their date of birth. Instead of calculating the difference between dates manually, you can enter your birth date and get your age in years, months and days instantly.

The calculator can also show the approximate number of days until your next birthday and the approximate total number of days you have been alive.

How to Use This Age Calculator

You can calculate your age in just a few simple steps:

  • Enter your date of birth in the date field.
  • Click the "Calculate Age" button.
  • Your age will be displayed in years, months and days.
  • You can also see information about your next birthday.

Why Use an Online Age Calculator?

Calculating an exact age manually can be difficult because months have different numbers of days and leap years can affect date calculations. An online age calculator makes the process faster and easier.

This tool is useful when you want to quickly calculate your current age for everyday purposes, forms, applications, planning or general information.

How Age Is Calculated

Your age is calculated by comparing your date of birth with the current date. The calculator first determines the difference in years, then accounts for the remaining months and days.

The result is displayed in the format of years, months and days so that you can understand your exact age more easily.
