What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a simple calculation that compares a person's weight with their height. BMI is commonly used as a screening measure to classify weight categories.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

For example, if a person weighs 70 kg and is 1.75 meters tall:

BMI = 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.86

Standard Adult BMI Categories

BMI Range Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Normal Weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 and above Obesity
Important: BMI is a general screening tool and does not directly measure body fat or overall health. Muscle mass, age, sex, body composition and other factors can affect how BMI should be interpreted.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For standard adult BMI categories, a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is generally classified as normal weight.

How is BMI calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

Can I calculate BMI in pounds and feet?

Yes. Select the Imperial option and enter your weight in pounds and height in feet.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI is a screening measure and may not accurately represent body composition for every individual, especially people with high muscle mass.

Does BMI directly measure body fat?

No. BMI does not directly measure body fat. It is a screening measure based on height and weight.
