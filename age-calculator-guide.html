What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is an online tool that calculates the difference between a person's date of birth and another date, such as today's date. The result can be expressed in completed years, months and days.

Instead of manually counting calendar years and accounting for different month lengths and leap years, an age calculator performs these calculations automatically.

How Is Age Calculated?

The basic idea is to compare the date of birth with the date on which the age is being calculated.

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Select the date on which you want to calculate your age.
  3. The calculator compares both dates.
  4. The result shows your completed age.
Age = Difference Between Date of Birth and Selected Date

Age Calculation Example

Suppose someone was born on 10 January 2000 and the calculation date is 10 January 2025.

Result:
The person has completed 25 years.

If the calculation date were before 10 January 2025, the person would still be 24 years old because their 25th birthday had not occurred yet.

Age in Years, Months and Days

Sometimes simply knowing the number of completed years is not enough. An age calculator can also show the remaining months and days.

Example:
Date of birth: 15 March 2000
Calculation date: 20 June 2025

Approximate result: 25 years, 3 months and 5 days.

The exact result depends on the calendar dates involved, including the number of days in each month and leap-year rules.

Age calculations are commonly useful when filling out forms, applications, registrations and other documents where a person's age is required.

Age may also be relevant when checking eligibility requirements, planning birthdays, calculating milestones or simply finding the exact difference between two dates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated?

Age is calculated by comparing the date of birth with the date on which the age is being calculated and determining the completed years, months and days.

Can I calculate my age in years, months and days?

Yes. An age calculator can calculate the difference between two dates and present the result in years, months and days.

Why does age change on a birthday?

A completed year of age is reached on the person's birthday. Before that birthday occurs, the next full year has not yet been completed.
