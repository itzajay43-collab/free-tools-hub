Free Online Case Converter

FreeToolsHub Case Converter is a free online text formatting tool that lets you quickly change the capitalization of your text. Convert text into uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case and other formats without manually editing every word.

Available Text Case Options

  • UPPERCASE: Converts every letter to capital letters.
  • lowercase: Converts every letter to lowercase.
  • Title Case: Capitalizes the first letter of each word.
  • Sentence case: Capitalizes the first letter of each sentence.
  • Alternating Case: Alternates between lowercase and uppercase letters.
  • Inverse Case: Changes uppercase letters to lowercase and lowercase letters to uppercase.

How to Use the Case Converter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. Select the case format you want.
  3. Your text will be converted instantly.
  4. Click Copy Text to copy the converted text.
  5. Click Clear to start with new text.

Who Can Use a Case Converter?

This tool is useful for students, writers, bloggers, developers, content creators, social media managers, marketers and anyone who needs to quickly change the capitalization of text.

Why Use an Online Case Converter?

Manually changing the capitalization of a large amount of text can take time. A case converter can transform your text in seconds, helping you format titles, paragraphs, headings, captions and other content more efficiently.
