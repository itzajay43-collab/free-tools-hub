What is a Time Zone Converter?

A Time Zone Converter helps you convert a specific date and time from one time zone to another. It is useful when scheduling meetings, online classes, international calls, travel plans and events across different countries.

How to Use This Time Zone Converter

  1. Select the date and time you want to convert.
  2. Select the original time zone.
  3. Select the destination time zone.
  4. Click Convert Time.
  5. The converted local date and time will be displayed instantly.

Why Do Time Zones Change?

Different regions of the world use different local times. Some countries also observe daylight saving time, which can change the difference between two time zones during parts of the year.

This converter uses your browser's built-in international time-zone data to calculate and display the conversion.
