What is Length Conversion?

Length conversion is the process of changing a measurement from one unit of distance to another. Different countries and industries use different units such as meters, kilometers, feet, inches and miles.

This free length converter makes it easy to convert common length and distance measurements without manually calculating conversion factors.

Supported Length Units

  • Meter (m)
  • Kilometer (km)
  • Centimeter (cm)
  • Millimeter (mm)
  • Mile (mi)
  • Yard (yd)
  • Foot (ft)
  • Inch (in)

Common Length Conversion Formulas
