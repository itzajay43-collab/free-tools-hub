What is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter is an online tool that changes a measurement from one unit to another. For example, you can convert kilometers to miles, kilograms to pounds, Celsius to Fahrenheit, liters to gallons and many other units.

Available Conversions

  • Length conversion
  • Weight and mass conversion
  • Temperature conversion
  • Area conversion
  • Volume conversion
  • Time conversion

Length Conversion

Length units are commonly used to measure distance. This converter supports units such as meters, kilometers, centimeters, millimeters, inches, feet, yards and miles.

Example:

1 kilometer = 1,000 meters.

Weight Conversion

Weight or mass can be converted between kilograms, grams, milligrams, pounds and ounces.

Example:

1 kilogram = 1,000 grams.

Temperature Conversion

Temperature can be converted between Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin using standard temperature conversion formulas.

Example:

0°C = 32°F.

Area Conversion

Area units can be converted between square meters, square kilometers, square feet, square yards, acres and hectares.

Volume Conversion

Volume conversion can be useful for liquids and other measurements. Supported units include liters, milliliters, gallons, cubic meters and cubic feet.

Time Conversion

Convert between seconds, minutes, hours, days and weeks quickly.

Tip: Select the conversion category, enter a value, choose the original unit and then choose the unit you want to convert to. The result will be calculated instantly.
