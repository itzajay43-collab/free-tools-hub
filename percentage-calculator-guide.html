What Is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The word percentage means "per hundred" and the symbol used for percentage is %.

Percentages are commonly used for discounts, exam marks, statistics, business calculations, interest, price changes and many everyday calculations.

Basic Percentage Formula

The basic formula for finding what percentage one number represents of another number is:

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Percentage Calculation Example

Suppose you scored 45 marks out of 60. To find your percentage:

Percentage = (45 ÷ 60) × 100

Percentage = 75%

Therefore, a score of 45 out of 60 is equal to 75%.

How to Find a Percentage of a Number

Sometimes you may want to find a specific percentage of a number. For example, to calculate 20% of 500:

20% of 500 = (20 ÷ 100) × 500 = 100

Therefore, 20% of 500 is 100.

How to Calculate Percentage Increase

Percentage increase tells you how much a value has increased compared with its original value.

Percentage Increase = ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100
Example:

Original value = 200
New value = 250

Increase = 250 − 200 = 50

Percentage Increase = (50 ÷ 200) × 100
= 25%

So, the value increased by 25%.

How to Calculate Percentage Decrease

Percentage decrease tells you how much a value has decreased compared with its original value.

Percentage Decrease = ((Original Value − New Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100
Example:

Original price = ₹1,000
New price = ₹800

Decrease = ₹1,000 − ₹800 = ₹200

Percentage Decrease = (200 ÷ 1,000) × 100
= 20%

Therefore, the price decreased by 20%.
