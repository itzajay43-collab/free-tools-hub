Free Online Typing Speed Test

FreeToolsHub Typing Speed Test helps you measure how fast and accurately you can type. The test calculates your typing speed in Words Per Minute (WPM), along with accuracy and errors.

How to Use the Typing Speed Test

  1. Click Start Test.
  2. Read the sample text displayed above the typing box.
  3. Type the text as accurately as possible.
  4. The timer runs for 60 seconds.
  5. View your WPM, accuracy, errors and correct words when the test ends.

What Is WPM?

WPM stands for Words Per Minute. It is a common measurement used to describe typing speed. A standard typing calculation generally considers five characters as one word.

How Is Typing Speed Calculated?

WPM is estimated from the number of correctly typed characters during the test.

WPM = Correct Characters ÷ 5 ÷ Time in Minutes

How Is Accuracy Calculated?

Accuracy is calculated by comparing your correctly typed characters with the total number of characters you entered.

Accuracy = Correct Characters ÷ Total Typed Characters × 100

Typing Speed Tips

  • Practice regularly instead of typing only occasionally.
  • Focus on accuracy before trying to type faster.
  • Use proper finger positioning.
  • Try to look at the screen instead of the keyboard.
  • Practice common words and sentences.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this typing test free?

What does WPM mean?

WPM means Words Per Minute. It represents the approximate number of standard five-character words you can type in one minute.

What is a good typing speed?

A typing speed of around 40 WPM is often considered a useful general typing speed, while faster speeds can be beneficial for professional typing-intensive work. Accuracy is also important.

Does the test measure accuracy?

Yes. The test compares your typed characters with the sample text and calculates an accuracy percentage.
