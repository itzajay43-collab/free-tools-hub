How to Calculate BMI

What Is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a screening measure that uses a person's weight and height to produce a numerical value.

BMI can provide a quick way to compare a person's weight with their height, but it should not be treated as a complete assessment of individual health.

Important: BMI is a screening measure. It does not directly measure body fat and may not accurately represent health for every person, including people with high muscle mass or certain individual circumstances.

BMI Formula

When using kilograms and metres, BMI is calculated using the following formula:

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ [Height (m) × Height (m)]

In other words, divide your weight in kilograms by your height in metres squared.

Example of BMI Calculation

Suppose a person weighs 70 kg and is 1.75 metres tall.

BMI = 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75)

BMI = 70 ÷ 3.0625

BMI ≈ 22.86

The calculated BMI is approximately 22.86.

Common Adult BMI Categories

The following are commonly used BMI categories for adults:

BMI Range Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Healthy weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 or above Obesity

These categories are general adult screening ranges and should not be interpreted as a diagnosis.

How to Calculate BMI Using Pounds and Inches

BMI can also be calculated using weight in pounds and height in inches.

BMI = 703 × Weight (lb) ÷ [Height (in) × Height (in)]

For example, if someone weighs 154 pounds and is 68 inches tall:

BMI = 703 × 154 ÷ (68 × 68)

BMI ≈ 23.39

Why Calculate BMI?

BMI can be useful as a quick screening measurement and can help people understand how their weight relates to their height.

  • Quickly calculate a BMI value.
  • Compare weight with height.
  • Understand common adult BMI categories.
  • Use the result as a starting point for further discussion.

Limitations of BMI

BMI does not directly measure body fat. Two people can have the same BMI while having different amounts of muscle and body fat.

Factors such as muscle mass, body composition, age and individual circumstances can affect how meaningful a BMI value is.

For children and teenagers, BMI interpretation is different and generally takes age and sex into account rather than simply applying adult categories.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a screening measure calculated using weight and height.

How is BMI calculated?

With metric measurements, divide weight in kilograms by height in metres squared.

What is the BMI formula?

The metric formula is BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)².

What are the common adult BMI categories?

Common adult categories are underweight below 18.5, healthy weight from 18.5 to below 25, overweight from 25 to below 30, and obesity at 30 or above.

Is BMI a complete measure of health?

No. BMI is a screening measure and does not directly measure body fat or fully describe an individual's health.
