Free Online Character Counter

FreeToolsHub Character Counter is a fast and simple online tool for counting characters in any text. It can count total characters, characters with and without spaces, letters, numbers, special characters, words and lines.

How to Use the Character Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text box.
  2. The character statistics update automatically.
  3. Check characters with or without spaces.
  4. Check letters, numbers and special characters.
  5. Use the Copy button to copy your text.
  6. Click Clear to start again.

What Does This Character Counter Calculate?

  • Total Characters: Total number of characters in the text.
  • With Spaces: Characters including spaces and line breaks.
  • Without Spaces: Characters after removing whitespace.
  • Letters: Alphabetic characters such as A-Z and a-z.
  • Numbers: Numeric characters from 0 to 9.
  • Special Characters: Characters that are not letters, numbers or whitespace.
  • Words: Words separated by whitespace.
  • Lines: Number of lines in the entered text.

Who Can Use a Character Counter?

Character counters are useful for students, writers, bloggers, social media creators, copywriters, marketers, SEO professionals and anyone who needs to stay within a specific character limit.

Why Count Characters?

Some websites, forms and social media platforms use character limits. A character counter makes it easy to check the length of your content before publishing or submitting it.
