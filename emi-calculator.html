What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed amount a borrower generally pays every month to repay a loan over a specified period.

An EMI generally consists of two components: the principal amount and the interest charged on the outstanding loan balance.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

Where:

  • P = Principal loan amount
  • r = Monthly interest rate
  • n = Number of monthly installments

How is Monthly Interest Rate Calculated?

The annual interest rate is converted into a monthly decimal rate before applying the EMI formula.

Monthly Rate = Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an EMI?

EMI means Equated Monthly Installment. It is the amount generally paid every month toward a loan.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI is calculated using the loan principal, monthly interest rate and total number of monthly installments.

Can I use this calculator for a home loan?

Yes. You can use this EMI calculator to estimate home loan EMI by entering the loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure.

Can I calculate car loan EMI?

Yes. Enter the car loan amount, annual interest rate and repayment period to estimate your monthly car loan EMI.

Does a longer loan tenure reduce EMI?

A longer tenure generally reduces the monthly EMI, but it can increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

What happens if the interest rate is 0%?

If the interest rate is 0%, the loan amount is divided equally across the number of monthly installments.
