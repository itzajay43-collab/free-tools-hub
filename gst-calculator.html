What is GST?

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax. It is an indirect tax applied to the supply of many goods and services. Businesses may need to calculate GST when selling products or providing services.

How to Add GST

When you know the price before GST, use the following formula to calculate the GST amount:

GST Amount = Original Price × GST Rate ÷ 100

Then calculate the final price:

Final Price = Original Price + GST Amount

How to Remove GST

If the displayed price already includes GST, you cannot simply subtract the GST percentage. Instead, use the following formula:

Original Price = GST-Inclusive Price ÷ (1 + GST Rate ÷ 100)

The GST amount is then:

GST Amount = GST-Inclusive Price − Original Price

GST Calculation Example

Suppose the price before GST is ₹1,000 and the GST rate is 18%.

GST = ₹1,000 × 18 ÷ 100 = ₹180
Final Price = ₹1,000 + ₹180 = ₹1,180

Therefore, the GST-inclusive price is ₹1,180.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate GST?

Multiply the price before GST by the GST percentage and divide by 100. Add the GST amount to the original price to get the GST-inclusive price.

How do I remove GST from an amount?

Divide the GST-inclusive amount by 1 plus the GST rate divided by 100. The difference between the total and original amount is the GST.

What is 18% GST on ₹1,000?

18% GST on ₹1,000 is ₹180, making the total price ₹1,180.

What are common GST rates?

Common GST rates include 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%, although the applicable rate depends on the specific product or service.
