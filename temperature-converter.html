What is Temperature Conversion?

Temperature conversion is the process of converting a temperature from one measurement scale to another. The most commonly used temperature scales are Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin.

Celsius

Celsius (°C) is widely used around the world for everyday temperature measurements. Water freezes at 0°C and boils at 100°C under standard atmospheric pressure.

Fahrenheit

Fahrenheit (°F) is commonly used for everyday temperature measurements in the United States. Water freezes at 32°F and boils at 212°F under standard atmospheric pressure.

Kelvin

Kelvin (K) is an absolute temperature scale commonly used in science. Kelvin does not use the degree symbol.

Temperature Conversion Formulas

Fahrenheit = (Celsius × 9/5) + 32
Celsius = (Fahrenheit − 32) × 5/9
Kelvin = Celsius + 273.15
Celsius = Kelvin − 273.15

Temperature Conversion Examples

Example 1: Celsius to Fahrenheit

25°C × 9/5 + 32 = 77°F

Example 2: Fahrenheit to Celsius

(98.6°F − 32) × 5/9 = 37°C

Example 3: Celsius to Kelvin

25°C + 273.15 = 298.15 K

Example 4: Kelvin to Celsius

300 K − 273.15 = 26.85°C

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 0°C in Fahrenheit?

0°C equals 32°F.

What is 100°C in Fahrenheit?

100°C equals 212°F.

What is 25°C in Fahrenheit?

25°C equals 77°F.

What is absolute zero?

Absolute zero is 0 Kelvin, which equals −273.15°C or approximately −459.67°F.
