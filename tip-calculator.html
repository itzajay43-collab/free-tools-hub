Free Online Tip Calculator

FreeToolsHub Tip Calculator helps you quickly calculate a tip, total restaurant bill and per-person payment. Enter your bill, select a tip percentage and choose how many people are sharing the bill.

How to Use the Tip Calculator

  1. Enter the total bill amount.
  2. Enter the number of people sharing the bill.
  3. Select a tip percentage such as 10%, 15% or 20%.
  4. Click Calculate Tip or enter your values directly.
  5. View the tip, total bill and per-person amounts.

Tip Calculation Formula

Tip Amount = Bill Amount × Tip Percentage ÷ 100

Total Bill = Bill Amount + Tip Amount

Amount Per Person = Total Bill ÷ Number of People

Example

If your restaurant bill is ₹2,000 and you choose a 15% tip, the tip amount is ₹300. The total bill becomes ₹2,300. If four people share the bill equally, each person pays ₹575.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a tip?

A tip is an additional amount paid to service staff in appreciation for their service. Whether and how much to tip depends on the country, establishment and local customs.

How is a 15% tip calculated?

Multiply the bill by 15 and divide the result by 100. For example, a ₹1,000 bill with a 15% tip gives a ₹150 tip.

Can I split the bill between multiple people?

Yes. Enter the number of people and the calculator will show the estimated tip and total amount for each person.

Can I enter a custom tip percentage?

Yes. You can enter any tip percentage from 0% to 100%.

Is this tip calculator free?

Yes. FreeToolsHub Tip Calculator is free to use online.
