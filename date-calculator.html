Free Online Date Calculator

This free date calculator helps you calculate the number of days between two dates and find a date by adding or subtracting a specific number of days.

How to Calculate the Difference Between Two Dates

Select a start date and an end date in the Date Difference calculator. Click Calculate Difference to see the total number of elapsed calendar days between the two dates.

For example, from January 1 to January 31, the difference is 30 days.

How to Add or Subtract Days

Select your starting date, choose either Add Days or Subtract Days, enter the number of days and click Calculate Date.

  • Add days to find a future date.
  • Subtract days to find a previous date.
  • Leap years are handled automatically.
  • The calculator works directly in your browser.

Are Months and Years Exact?

The total days result is based on calendar dates. The displayed month and year values are approximate conversions based on the total number of days because calendar months have different lengths.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this date calculator free?

Yes. FreeToolsHub's date calculator is completely free to use.

How do I calculate days between two dates?

Select the start date and end date and click Calculate Difference.

Can I find a date after a certain number of days?

Yes. Use the Add / Subtract Days option, choose Add Days and enter the number of days you want to add.

Does this calculator handle leap years?

Yes. Calendar calculations automatically account for leap years.

Are the month and year calculations exact?

No. Month and year values are approximate conversions based on the total number of days. The total day count is the primary result.
