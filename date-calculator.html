Free Online Date Calculator

FreeToolsHub Date Calculator helps you find the number of days between two dates and calculate a future or past date by adding or subtracting a specific number of days.

How to Calculate the Difference Between Two Dates

  1. Choose the Date Difference tab.
  2. Select the starting date.
  3. Select the ending date.
  4. Click Calculate Difference.
  5. View the total days, weeks, months and years.

How to Add or Subtract Days

  1. Open the Add / Subtract Days tab.
  2. Select a starting date.
  3. Choose Add Days or Subtract Days.
  4. Enter the number of days.
  5. Click Calculate Date.

How Date Difference Is Calculated

The calculator measures the elapsed time between the selected dates. The day difference is calculated using calendar dates, while the week, month and year figures are provided as approximate conversions.

Example

If the start date is January 1 and the end date is January 31, the elapsed difference is 30 days. Adding 30 days to January 1 also produces January 31.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this date calculator free?

Yes. FreeToolsHub Date Calculator is free to use online.

Can I calculate the number of days between two dates?

Yes. Enter a start date and end date in the Date Difference section to calculate the elapsed number of days.

Can I find a date after a certain number of days?

Yes. Use the Add / Subtract Days section to calculate a future or past date.

Does the calculator handle leap years?

Yes. The calculator uses JavaScript date calculations, which account for leap years and different month lengths.

Are the month and year results exact?

The month and year values shown in the date-difference section are approximate conversions based on the total number of days. Calendar month and year lengths vary.
